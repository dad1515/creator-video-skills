# Positioning Evaluation

Score each dimension from 1 to 5 and cite the evidence behind the score.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience specificity | Everyone or topic-only | Recognizable segment | Clear identity, stage, pain, and desired result |
| Creator credibility | Unsupported claim | Relevant experience | Concrete work, cases, methods, or results |
| Problem urgency | Vague interest | Recurring frustration | Specific costly or blocking problem |
| Transformation clarity | "Learn/improve" | General output | Observable completed result with a quality bar |
| Differentiation | Generic category | Some angle | Defensible combination of expertise, method, and voice |
| Content runway | Fewer than 10 topics | Roughly 20-40 | At least 50 coherent topics across 3-5 pillars |
| Business alignment | No next step | General monetization | Clear audience-to-offer path without forcing promotion |
| Spoken-content fit | Abstract or reference-heavy | Mixed | Benefits from story, demonstration, opinion, or explanation |

## Acceptance Rules

- Do not recommend a strategy with any score of 1.
- Treat audience specificity, creator credibility, and transformation clarity as critical. Each must score at least 3.
- A recommended strategy should total at least 28/40 and state its weakest dimension.
- Do not fabricate market size or demand. Label untested demand as an assumption.

## Distinctness Test

Compare each pair of strategies across:

1. Primary audience
2. Core promise
3. Creator role
4. Content-pillar mix
5. Business path

At least two dimensions must materially differ. Otherwise regenerate the weaker strategy.

## Interview Test

Consider discovery successful when:

- No more than eight questions were needed.
- The user understands why each question was asked.
- The user can explain the difference between the three strategies.
- The selected strategy yields five clearly aligned sample topics.
- The user can identify at least one topic or audience that should be excluded.
